GroundWorks Art Lab

Welcome!

At Groundworks Art Lab, art is for everyone. Everyone who feels a call to create, express an idea, or channel an emotion is welcome to come through our doors and make something all their own. Whether you want to find your flow in ceramics or bang away in our blacksmith shop, we carve out space for people to connect—to themselves, to elemental materials, to raw creativity, and to one another. From first-timers to full-blown professionals, everyone is invited to explore, experiment, and make something real. We believe art unlocks something essential in each of us. And we’re here to make sure everyone can get their hands on it.
